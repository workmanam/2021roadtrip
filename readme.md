### Summer 2021 Roadtrip

This map shows points of interest along the route of a roadtrip I went on in June and July 2021. The start point of the roadtrip was my hometown of Moyock, North Carolina and we went as far west as the Grand Canyon before heading back on a different route. Pictures I took of each place are included in the tooltips.

##
### Places Included
- Arches National Park, Utah
- Bass Pro Shop Pyramid, Tennessee
- Cadillac Ranch, Texas
- Dinosaur National Monument, Colorado
- Edgar Evins State Park, Tennessee
- Grand Canyon, Arizona
- Kansas City, Missouri
- Lake Celina, Indiana
- Lake Eufaula, Oklahoma
- Meteor Crater, Arizona
- Moyock, North Carolina
- Navajo Bridge, Arizona
- Petroglyph National Monument, New Mexico
- Pike's Peak, Colorado
- Santa Rosa Lake State Park, New Mexico
- Walnut Canyon, Arizona
